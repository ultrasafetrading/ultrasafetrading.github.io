HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a browser, or upload the complete folder to your web hosting public_html directory.
3. Keep the folder structure unchanged.

IMPORTANT SETUP NOTES
- Bootstrap 5.3.3 and Bootstrap Icons are loaded from public CDNs, so an internet connection is required for those assets.
- The contact forms use the visitor's default email application (mailto). They do not store or submit form data to a server. For production, connect a secure form backend/CRM and configure spam protection.
- The enquiry popup appears after 9 seconds once per browser session on pages other than Contact. It can be adjusted in js/main.js.
- WhatsApp and call buttons are included site-wide. WhatsApp currently opens a chat to +971 54 786 5428.
- Replace or expand the sample blog posts with approved company articles before launch.
- Verify all business details, collection arrangements, data destruction capabilities, environmental claims and applicable permits before publishing. No certifications or regulatory approvals are claimed in this template.
- Add a sitemap.xml and robots.txt, submit the sitemap in Google Search Console, and configure hosting HTTPS before launch.
- The supplied logo is used as provided. A transparent/high-resolution vector logo would improve sharpness in some placements.
